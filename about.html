Who We Are

Connecting ambition to global opportunity.

Fly Connect Global is an international education, immigration and travel consultancy helping individuals, families and businesses access opportunities across the world.

Since 2019, we have helped people successfully move abroad to study, work, reunite with family and begin new chapters. With our global headquarters in the United States and offices in Lagos and Abeokuta, we combine international reach with the personal support of a team that understands your journey.

Your dream is personal. Our role is to help you find a clear and practical path towards it.
